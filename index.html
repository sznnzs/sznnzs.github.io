🐳 About Me

I am a first-year Ph.D. student at The Hong Kong Polytechnic University, supervised by Prof. Yongqi Li and co-supervised by Prof. Wenjie Li, where I am supported by the PolyU Presidential PhD Fellowship Scheme (PPPFS).

I obtained my M.S. (2023-2026) in Computer Science at Tsinghua University with Outstanding Thesis Award, supervised by Prof. Yong Jiang and Prof. Qing Li. I received my B.S. (2019-2023) in Computer Science and Technology from Jilin University with the Wang Xianghao Scholarship.

My research interests mainly include efficient and trustworthy large language model reasoning, language model collaboration, and intelligent network systems. If you find my work interesting, feel free to contact me for discussion or collaboration.
